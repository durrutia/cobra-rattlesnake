### Born somewhere between a night market and a mountain climb.

Cobra RattleSnake is a fictional hot-sauce company with a simple philosophy: **great sauce should make you want another bite, not another glass of milk.**

Our imaginary kitchen draws inspiration from Taiwan&apos;s punchy flavors—chili, garlic, citrus, soy, sesame, and a little sweet funk—then takes them on two wheels.

The result is sauce for breakfast dumplings, beef noodles, fried chicken, rice bowls, grilled vegetables, and anything else that looks like it needs a little danger.

### The ride

Our label art celebrates Taiwan&apos;s legendary cycling culture: steep green mountains, compact city streets, glowing night markets, and the unmistakable rhythm of a bicycle freewheel.

No trophies required. Just bring an appetite.
